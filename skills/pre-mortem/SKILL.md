# Pre-mortem

1. **Frame:** "It is 12 months from now. This failed. What happened?"
2. **Generate causes** across categories:
   - Desirability: problem not painful enough, wrong customer, solution not wanted.
   - Viability: price too low, CAC too high, margins eaten by returns/fees/inference, no repeat.
   - Feasibility: quality bar (esp. AI) not reached, dependency changed terms, too slow to build.
   - Distribution: no channel reaches them cheaply; a platform changed rules.
   - Competition: incumbent ships it as a feature; customers stick with doing nothing.
   - Founder/team: time, focus, runway, motivation, skills gap.
   - External: regulation, seasonality, macro.
3. **Rank** by likelihood × severity. Keep the top 5.
4. **Early warning signal** for each: what we would see in the next 4 weeks if this is happening.
5. **Cheapest test or mitigation** for the top 2.
6. **Base rate:** how often ventures/tactics of this type fail and the most common cause.
