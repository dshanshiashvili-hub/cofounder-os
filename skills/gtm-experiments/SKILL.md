# GTM experiments

## Positioning first (Dunford)
1. Competitive alternatives (including doing nothing). 2. Unique attributes. 3. Value those attributes enable. 4. Best-fit customer who cares most. 5. Market category that makes the value obvious. Write a one-line positioning statement before any channel test.

## Channel selection (Weinberg, Bullseye)
- List candidate channels broadly (e.g. direct outreach, communities, creators/UGC, SEO/content, social organic, paid social, partnerships, marketplaces, PR, events, referrals).
- Pick 2–3 for cheap tests (≤ 1 week, small budget each). Measure cost per qualified lead or per commitment, not clicks.
- Concentrate on the winner. Revisit only when it saturates.

## Test patterns
- **Landing page / fake door:** one promise, one CTA, price visible when testing willingness to pay. Track visit → CTA → commitment.
- **Preorder / deposit:** strongest pre-product evidence. Refund promptly if you don't ship.
- **Manual sales / outreach:** 20–50 targeted messages; measure reply → call → commitment.
- **Creator / UGC test:** small paid or gifted collaborations; measure attributable commitments, not views.

## Rules
- Thresholds on the card before launch.
- Same audience quality across variants; don't compare a warm list to cold ads.
- No dark patterns, fake reviews, or fake scarcity.
